THE FOUNDRY LOFT - EVENT AGREEMENT

Client: Northwind Partners
Event: Annual Client Summit

1. DATE AND TIME. The Event will be held on Thursday, February 18, 2027. The space
is reserved from 6:00 PM to 9:00 PM. Client access for setup begins at 4:00 PM.

2. SPACE. The Main Hall is an indoor space with a maximum seated capacity of 120
guests. Standing receptions may accommodate more, subject to written approval from
the venue manager.

3. FOOD AND BEVERAGE. Client agrees to a food and beverage minimum of $9,000,
exclusive of tax and a 22% service charge. Menus are priced per person.

4. GUARANTEED HEADCOUNT. Client must provide a final guaranteed headcount no later
than February 4, 2027. After that date the guarantee may be increased, subject to
capacity, but not reduced. An attrition allowance of 10% applies before that date.

5. CANCELLATION. Client may cancel without penalty until January 19, 2027.
Cancellations after that date forfeit the full food and beverage minimum.

6. PARKING. Valet parking is available on site and billed per vehicle.

Signed: R. Alvarez, Events Manager, The Foundry Loft
